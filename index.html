03 / The model

Buyback parity simulator

Conditional scenario Not a prediction, and not a supply-neutrality threshold.

Formula

Required daily volume (USD) = daily issuance (NEAR) × NEAR price (USD) ÷ (net revenue capture rate × buyback allocation). Rates are converted to decimals: bps ÷ 10,000 and % ÷ 100.
